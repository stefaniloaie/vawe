How Vawe detects an ocean event

By default, Vawe groups consecutive observed wave heights at or above 3 m into a high-wave event. The threshold is configurable in the interactive dashboard; it is not an official warning threshold.

What significant wave height means

It is a statistical measure that approximates the average height of the highest third of measured waves in an observation period. It is not the height of every individual wave.

Source and safety

Data provider: NOAA National Data Buoy Center. Vawe shows observations, not official safety guidance. Check NOAA/NWS marine alerts before making on-water decisions.
